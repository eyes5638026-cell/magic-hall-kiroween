# Magic Hall — 90-second demo script

Prepared: 2026-11-20

**Target duration:** 90 seconds  
**Language:** English  
**Narration:** 167 words  
**Destination:** Public YouTube video

Record the working application. Leave space for clicks and a brief music sample. Show the sample-data label when the origin story is opened.

## 0–12 seconds

**On screen:** Show the working hall at its normal starting state. Move the pointer toward the candles.

**Narration / spoken subtitle:**

Magic Hall gives art, music, and the stories behind them a place to live. Instead of opening another folder, you enter a candlelit room.

**Short caption:** Magic Hall — art, music, and their stories

## 12–28 seconds

**On screen:** Click a gold candle, show a sample artwork, then use the actual change-picture control once.

**Narration / spoken subtitle:**

Choose a gold candle to open a picture. Each work gets space to be seen. You can move to another piece without leaving the hall.

**Short caption:** Gold candles open art

## 28–43 seconds

**On screen:** Return to the hall, select a blue candle, let the sample music play briefly, and demonstrate pause or volume.

**Narration / spoken subtitle:**

Choose a blue candle to play a song. Pause it, change the volume, or discover another track. The color of each candle tells you what it holds.

**Short caption:** Blue candles play music

## 43–58 seconds

**On screen:** Open the source-story panel and show one short fictional conversation. Keep the fictional-demo label legible.

**Narration / spoken subtitle:**

A work can also lead back to its origin. Here, a short conversation restores the idea behind the image or music. The public demo uses fictional conversations and newly created sample media.

**Short caption:** Revisit the idea behind a work

## 58–73 seconds

**On screen:** Briefly show the public README and truthful Kiro contribution record, then return to the working application.

**Narration / spoken subtitle:**

Kiro Crew designed and coded the original hall during this hackathon. It uses HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Canvas. The public edition keeps the same interaction with a small, shareable collection.

**Short caption:** Built with Kiro Crew

## 73–90 seconds

**On screen:** Finish with the hall and one final artwork. Show the verified application and GitHub URLs after they exist.

**Narration / spoken subtitle:**

This is our Costume Contest entry: a magical interface that helps you choose and revisit creative memories. The application and its sample collection are available for judges to try.

**Short caption:** Light a candle. Rediscover a creative memory.
